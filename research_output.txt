Answer: ### Jesus of Nazareth (c. 6 BC – AD 30/33)

**Historical Context**
- 1st‑century Jewish preacher and religious leader in Roman‑controlled Judea.
- Virtually all modern scholars agree he existed, though details of his life are debated.

**Life and Ministry**
- Baptized by John the Baptist; spent 40 days in the wilderness before beginning public ministry.
- Taught in parables, performed healings and miracles, and gathered twelve apostles.
- Primary accounts are the four canonical Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, John).

**Crucifixion and Resurrection**
- Arrested in Jerusalem, tried before the Sanhedrin, handed to Pontius Pilate, and crucified.
- Christians believe his death was a sacrificial atonement.
- According to Christian doctrine, he rose from the dead on the third day (Resurrection) and later ascended to Heaven.

**Celebrations**
- Birth: Christmas (December 25).
- Crucifixion: Good Friday.
- Resurrection: Easter.

**Religious Perspectives**
- **Christianity**: Central figure; regarded as the incarnation of God the Son and the awaited Messiah.
- **Islam**: Recognized as the prophet ʿĪsā, a messiah born of the Virgin Mary, but not divine; Muslims deny his crucifixion, teaching that God raised him alive.
- **Judaism**: Rejects Jesus as the Messiah, citing unmet messianic prophecies and lack of divine status.

**Key Points**
- Jesus is a pivotal figure across multiple religions, each interpreting his nature and role differently.
- Historical consensus affirms his existence; theological interpretations vary widely.

## 9. Harvest & Post‑Harvest Handling
- **Harvest timing:** Fruit picked mature but still firm.
- **Ripening:** Ethylene‑induced at 20–25 °C for 2–5 days.
